Key takeaways
Scores cluster around the mid-6 range. Genres differ in average scores, but genre labels overlap because one title can belong to multiple genres. Audience size and ratings show a positive association in this dataset; this does not establish causation.

Data coverage and limitations
Based on anime_clean.csv only. Popularity is represented by membership counts (larger means more audience interest); popularity rank, where lower is more popular, is a separate metric. Review volume and review sentiment are not displayed because the review-count and sentiment datasets were not available. Genre comparisons use only genres with at least 100 rated titles. Associations are descriptive, not causal.
